MISSION OPERATIONAL PROBLEM

          

            During long-duration crewed interplanetary transit between Earth and Mars, radio transmission delay spans 4 to 22 minutes one-way (8 to 44 minutes round-trip). In acute crises—such as Solar Particle Event (SPE) radiation storms, insidious hypoxic leaks, or sudden rotational habitat despin shocks—real-time telemedicine consultation with Houston Flight Surgeons is physically impossible. Aegis-DeepSpace serves as an auditable, deterministic, multimodal edge Flight Surgeon safeguarding astronaut lives locally with zero reliance on cloud or Earth infrastructure.
