Mission operational problem

            

              During crewed transit between Earth and Mars, one-way radio propagation delay spans 4 to 22 minutes (8 to 44 minutes round-trip). In acute events—such as Solar Particle Event (SPE) radiation storms, insidious hypoxic leaks, or rotational habitat despin—real-time consultation with ground flight surgeons is impossible. Aegis-DeepSpace provides auditable, deterministic, multimodal clinical decision support locally on spacecraft hardware.
            

          

        


        
Four architectural pillars


        

          

            

              Pillar 1
              Environmental defense
            

            
Radiation safe-route

            

              Models spacecraft topology as a weighted graph (NetworkX) across 4 habitat compartments (Command Deck 20% shielded, Storm Shelter 95%, Science Lab 60%, Airlock 10%). Calculates compartment dose rates and runs Dijkstra pathfinding to minimize cumulative Sievert transit exposure during Solar Particle Events using NASA DONKI space weather feeds.
            

          


          

            

              Pillar 2
              Passive biometrics
            

            
Voice vitals

            

              Passive, non-invasive crew acoustic monitoring. Extracts 13-dimensional MFCC prosody via a 1D-CNN to detect vocal fatigue and sub-clinical hypoxia; generates transcripts using OpenAI Whisper STT (tiny.en); and evaluates mood valence and cognitive strain using DistilBERT NLP with personalized z-score drift tracking (μ ± σ).
            

          


          

            

              Pillar 3
              Predictive digital twin
            

            
Astro-Twin musculoskeletal

            

              Couples Frost's Mechanostat ODE with a Scikit-Learn Gradient Boosting Regressor trained on 180-day ARED mission data. Simulates 30-day forward bone mineral density (BMD) loss and prescribes compensatory countermeasures (work deficit tonnage, volume surge %, and squat/deadlift adjustments) following shelter confinement.
            

          


          

            

              Pillar 4
              Autonomous synthesis
            

            
Autonomous decision engine

            

              Deterministic risk fusion combining P1 + P2 + P3 into a composite score (0.05–0.99) with multi-signal synergy escalation (+0.10 to +0.20), closed-loop cross-pillar feedback (shelter confinement driving twin deconditioning), offline NASA-STD-3001 evidence retrieval, and counterfactual evacuation delay modeling.
